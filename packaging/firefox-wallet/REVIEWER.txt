EpixZone maintains EpixNet and Epix Wallet, an independent fork of Keplr Wallet by Chainapsis. Chainapsis does not endorse Epix Wallet. See epix-wallet/NOTICE and LICENSE. Sites can use window.keplr and the legacy isKeplr and isMetaMask flags. EIP-6963 discovery identifies “Epix Wallet” as zone.epix.wallet.

Sites request connection and signature approval separately. Host access runs the wallet provider and navigation checks. EpixNet’s address bar recognizes bare EPIX addresses. The wallet redirects bare EPIX hosts to .epix and leaves search-provider queries intact. Proxy, request blocking and native messaging support Tor routing, local node settings and Ledger devices. Storage holds wallet state, idle supports locking, alarms check node health, notifications report transactions, activeTab identifies the current site and scripting removes stale provider registrations. EpixNet/packaging/firefox-wallet/DATA-DISCLOSURE.md identifies recipients and requests made before signing.

Test in a fresh Firefox profile with a disposable, unfunded account.

1. Open the wallet and check the publisher and fork attribution before setup. Choose “Create a new wallet”, then “Create new recovery phrase”. Use the generated phrase and a disposable password of at least eight characters. Complete setup, lock and unlock.
2. From the extracted source directory, run:
   python3 -m http.server 8765 --bind 127.0.0.1 --directory EpixNet/packaging/firefox-wallet
   Open http://127.0.0.1:8765/review-site.html and click “Request test signature”. Connect the test account. Check the text, punctuation and spacing against the wallet prompt. Click the × next to Approve and confirm the page reports rejection.
3. In Settings open “Noble USDN balance and claims”. An unfunded account cannot claim or withdraw. Earlier dedicated Earn enrollment, deposit and terms screens were removed. Native staking is available separately.
4. Open blocklist.html at the extension’s moz-extension origin. Its tab title is “Epix Wallet”. Real phishing-list matches continue to open https://blocklist.keplr.app with the matched URL.

BUILD.txt describes rebuilding and comparing every file. After building the wallet libraries, run these tests from epix-wallet:
   yarn workspace @keplr-wallet/extension test --runInBand src/pages/sign/utils/message-preview.spec.ts src/pages/earn/overview/index.spec.tsx
